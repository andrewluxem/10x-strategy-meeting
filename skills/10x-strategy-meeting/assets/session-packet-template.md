# Session packet template

Fill every field. A field the user did not supply carries a visible assumption in place, never a silent guess. The packet ends at the capture plan; nothing follows it.

```
10x Strategy Session: [Business or team name, as supplied or described in the user's own words]
Objective: [One sentence naming the 10x question this session exists to answer]
Horizon: [Default 24 months unless the user set one; label the default]
Slot: [The user's meeting length; if none given, write "Length: Set by owner" and
  scale the block minutes as fractions of the total]
Owner: [Name]
Groups: [Attendee count split into groups of 3; name the split with commas, e.g. 9
  people, 3 groups of 3, or 10 people, two groups of 3 and one group of 4]
Roles per group: Idea Generator, Challenger, Note Taker
  [If a group has no Note Taker named, write "Note Taker needed"]

Framing, read aloud by the owner:
[Two or three sentences contrasting 10x with 10 percent for THIS business. The
question is "Why should we get there," not "How do we get there." Name the
assumption the team is allowed to drop.]

Blocks:
1. Framing and rules ([N] min) 10x not 10 percent, why-not-how, no feasibility
   objections during generation
2. Group ideation ([N] min) Groups of 3 work the starter questions
3. Present back ([N] min per group) The what and the why, any medium
4. Challenge and build ([N] min) Challengers probe feasibility and scale,
   building on ideas rather than dismissing them
5. Capture and route ([N] min) Note Takers consolidate; owner names next step

Starter questions, tailored to this business:
- Who are the customers?
- What makes people buy this, and what holds them back?
- How are customers adopting the nearest alternatives?
- What matters most to these customers when they decide?
- What will this market look like at the end of the horizon?
- Do we have, or can we find, data to support the idea?
- Can the idea scale?
[Replace generic nouns with the user's actual product, market, and customer.]

Capture plan:
Each Note Taker records every idea as What / Why / Discussion points, using
assets/capture-template.md. The owner collects all three sheets before the room
empties.
```

## Filled example

The slot below was supplied by the requester; the horizon was not, so it carries the default with its label.

```
10x Strategy Session: Big Dill Pickleball Co.
Objective: Find the ideas with the potential to grow the business tenfold, not ten percent.
Horizon: 24 months (default, none supplied)
Slot: 90 minutes
Owner: Katy
Groups: 9 people, 3 groups of 3
Roles per group: Idea Generator, Challenger, Note Taker
  Group 3 Note Taker needed

Framing, read aloud by the owner:
Ten percent thinking gets us a better paddle listing. Tenfold thinking asks what
it would take for pickleball gear to reach every rec center in the country. Drop
the assumption that we only sell what we manufacture. Ask why we should get
there, not how.

Blocks:
1. Framing and rules (10 min) 10x not 10 percent, why-not-how, no feasibility
   objections during generation
2. Group ideation (30 min) Groups of 3 work the starter questions
3. Present back (8 min per group) The what and the why, any medium
4. Challenge and build (15 min) Challengers probe feasibility and scale,
   building on ideas rather than dismissing them
5. Capture and route (10 min) Note Takers consolidate; owner names next step

Starter questions, tailored to this business:
- Who buys paddles today, and who plays without ever buying gear?
- What makes a player upgrade, and what holds casual players back?
- How are players adopting apparel and accessories from the nearest alternatives?
- What matters most to a league player choosing a paddle?
- What will recreational pickleball look like in 24 months?
- Do we have, or can we find, data to support the idea?
- Can the idea scale past our current channels?

Capture plan:
Each Note Taker records every idea as What / Why / Discussion points, using
assets/capture-template.md. The owner collects all three sheets before the room
empties.
```
