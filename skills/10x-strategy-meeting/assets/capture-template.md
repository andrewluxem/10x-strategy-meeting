# Capture template

One entry per idea, consolidated across groups. Ideas are recorded as presented; the skill labels gaps rather than filling them. The document ends at the routing table; nothing follows it.

```
10x Session Capture: [Business or team name]
Session date: [Date]
Owner: [Name]

Ideas:
1. [Idea name]
   What: [The idea in one or two sentences, as the group presented it]
   Why: [Why the business should get there; the customer or market case,
   or Not recorded when the notes carry none. Never reconstructed, even
   hedged as inferred]
   Discussion points: [Objections, builds, and open questions raised in the room.
   Carries only what the notes record; write "None noted" when they record none]
   Scale check: [Can it scale, per the room; "Not discussed" if it was not]
   Data check: [Supporting data named, or "Data needed"]

2. [Next idea, same fields]

Routing:
| Idea | Next step | Owner | By when |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Idea name] | [Split into team goals, park, or drop] | [Name or "Owner needed"] | [Date or "Date needed"] |
```

## Filled example

The example shows the awkward cases on purpose: one idea arrived with no scale discussion and no owner, and another arrived as a bare what, so its why reads Not recorded instead of a reconstruction.

```
10x Session Capture: Big Dill Pickleball Co.
Session date: November 12, 2026
Owner: Katy

Ideas:
1. League starter kits
   What: A bundled kit that lets any rec center start a beginner league, gear
   plus a season plan.
   Why: The market of players who never buy gear is larger than the market of
   players who do; leagues convert them.
   Discussion points: Challenger asked whether rec centers buy or players buy;
   room split. Build from group 2, add a coach referral program.
   Scale check: Yes per the room, rec centers number in the thousands
   Data check: Data needed

2. Paddle trade-in program
   What: Trade in any brand's paddle for credit toward ours.
   Why: Removes the biggest upgrade objection and takes a competitor's paddle
   out of play in the same transaction.
   Discussion points: Margin question raised and parked.
   Scale check: Not discussed
   Data check: Return-rate data exists from current exchanges

3. Weekend demo trailer
   What: Take a demo trailer to weekend tournaments.
   Why: Not recorded
   Discussion points: None noted
   Scale check: Not discussed
   Data check: Data needed

Routing:
| Idea | Next step | Owner | By when |
|---|---|---|---|
| League starter kits | Split into team goals | Katy | December 1, 2026 |
| Paddle trade-in program | Park pending margin review | Owner needed | Date needed |
| Weekend demo trailer | Park until group 3 supplies the why | Owner needed | Date needed |
```
